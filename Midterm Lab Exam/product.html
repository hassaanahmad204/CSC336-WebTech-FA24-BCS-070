Product Overview & Architecture

The 16-inch MacBook Pro blasts forward with M3 Max, an extraordinarily advanced chip that brings massive performance and capabilities for extreme workflows. Built on industry-leading 3-nanometer technology and featuring next-generation GPU architecture with hardware-accelerated ray tracing and mesh shading, it renders complex 3D scenes up to 2.5x faster than previous generation silicon.

Enjoy extreme dynamic range with the Liquid Retina XDR display, featuring 1,000 nits sustained full-screen brightness for HDR content, up to 1,600 nits peak brightness, and 10,000 mini-LEDs grouped into individually controlled local dimming zones. With ProMotion technology offering adaptive refresh rates up to 120Hz, scrolling and gaming feel extraordinarily fluid and responsive.

Connect everything you need with three Thunderbolt 4 ports, an HDMI port that supports 8K displays at up to 60Hz or 4K displays at 240Hz, an SDXC card slot for fast photo and video transfer, a 3.5mm headphone jack with advanced support for high-impedance headphones, and a dedicated MagSafe 3 charging port with fast-charge capability.
